# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: freelance clients — startup founders, agency leads, and product managers deciding whether to hire Himel for a frontend build or redesign. They evaluate quickly, often in one short visit, and shortlist candidates who feel technically premium and reliable.

Secondary: remote hiring managers and recruiters screening Himel for frontend engineer roles.

## Product Purpose

Himel's portfolio exists to win freelance projects and remote roles by presenting his frontend engineering craft credibly and memorably. Success is measured in inbound inquiries — client messages and interview requests.

## Positioning

Motion craftsmanship: award-caliber animation and interaction quality (GSAP/Lenis-level, awwwards-standard) delivered with engineering discipline. The site itself is the proof — a neighboring portfolio cannot truthfully copy this claim without shipping the same level of execution.

## Operating Context

Clients arrive from links or socials and evaluate within seconds; first viewport and first interaction matter. Much evaluation happens on mobile and touch devices. The portfolio must stay fast, smooth, and accessible under real-world conditions (prefers-reduced-motion, keyboard, screen readers).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frontend engineer, 4+ years, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh; available for freelance and remote work.
- Technical baseline: Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind, GSAP + Lenis animation stack.
- Binding visual constraints (user-volunteered): pure black & white — white background, black text, neutral grays only; no gradients, accent colors, or decorative noise. Fonts: Outfit (display), Montserrat (body), Geist Mono (labels/numbers).
- One section at a time; each section verified with lint, production build, and visual check before the next.
- Content is placeholder-only today (name, role, availability, email are real; projects, services, stack details are not). Never invent projects, clients, experience, or metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Himel. Role line: Frontend Engineer.
- Contact: hello@himel.dev; availability: "Available for freelance & remote".
- Voice: clean, concise, confident; no hype, no filler copy.
- Pure black & white identity and the three-font system above are binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: name, role, location, availability, email (in `src/lib/data.ts`).
- Placeholders only: projects, services, stack items, socials. No testimonials, case studies, metrics, or client names exist yet — none may be fabricated. Real content is expected later from Himel.

## Product Principles

1. The work proves the positioning — motion craft must be felt in the first seconds, not narrated.
2. Convert fast — the decision moment is short; contact must be one step away from the work.
3. Never fabricate evidence — ship placeholders until real content exists.
4. Restraint is a feature — every element must earn its place; silence and whitespace carry the premium feel.
5. Engineering integrity — performant, accessible, and correct on every device, not just desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site commits to `prefers-reduced-motion` support (animations disabled, content still visible), desktop-only custom cursor, and keyboard-accessible navigation. Mobile/touch is a first-class evaluation surface.
